Security

Signed-in as Admin. Sessions use HttpOnly cookies. Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes.

Users & PermissionsAdministrator only

Admins have full control. Viewers can watch live cameras and playback recordings. Admin may optionally grant Talk permission to individual viewers, but they cannot change cameras, users or system configuration.

Talk is off for all new Viewers unless explicitly allowed. It also must be enabled per compatible camera. The phone browser needs HTTPS for microphone access; VPN alone does not make HTTP a secure browser context. New viewers receive a password-change recommendation.

Network AccessVPN & web interface

Change the TCP port where PiNVR listens. Your Raspberry Pi will restart the web service and your browser must reconnect at the new address.

VPN access (recommended)

Use your Pi's VPN IP and the listening port, e.g. http://10.8.0.5:8080. Your VPN routes private traffic without requiring public router port forwarding.

Router port forwarding (advanced)

External and internal ports can be different (e.g. external 18443 → PiNVR internal 8080). Port forwarding does not provide HTTPS. Do not publish the HTTP administration interface directly on the internet; use a VPN or HTTPS reverse proxy with appropriate safeguards.

Mobile App Access

Open PiNVR on Android Chrome or iPhone Safari through your LAN or VPN, then Add to Home Screen. After a port change, update saved bookmarks and your PWA launch address.
